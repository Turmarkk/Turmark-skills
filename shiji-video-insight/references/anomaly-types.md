# Anomaly Types

Use these anomaly families as the first scan order when mining `shiji-kb`.

## 1. Narrative Silence

Use when a key person, office, relation, or ritual role should appear but is oddly absent.

Typical questions:

- Why is this role missing?
- Why is this relationship unnamed?
- Why does the text skip an expected institutional node?

Video value:

- strong curiosity
- naturally leads to structural interpretation

## 2. Numerical Anomaly

Use when troop counts, casualties, wealth, duration, or scale exceed normal expectations.

Typical questions:

- Is this number exaggerated?
- Does the logistics make sense?
- Is the number a propaganda artifact or a transmission distortion?

Video value:

- strong click potential
- easy to express in one sentence

## 3. Cross-Chapter Contradiction

Use when the same event, date, result, or attribution differs across chapters.

Typical questions:

- Which chapter says what?
- Is this a true contradiction, a perspective gap, or a scope mismatch?
- What does the contradiction reveal about sources?

Video value:

- strong "history detective" framing

## 4. Institutional Violation

Use when a title, succession pattern, ritual norm, or political arrangement breaks what should be normal.

Typical questions:

- Is this a temporary exception or a structural crack?
- Was the violation formal, practical, or narrative?

Video value:

- excellent for system-level explanation

## 5. Causal Reversal

Use when the common explanation feels too simple and the real driver is likely elsewhere.

Typical questions:

- What does the popular version blame?
- What structural factor is more likely the real cause?
- Is the visible cause just the trigger, not the engine?

Video value:

- ideal for counterintuitive insight videos

## 6. Source Transmission Distortion

Use when a story becomes more dramatic, moralized, or simplified as it is retold.

Typical questions:

- What is the earliest version?
- What layers may have been added later?
- Which details look literary rather than archival?

Video value:

- excellent for "how history was shaped" topics

## Selection Guidance

Prefer this order when choosing the lead topic:

1. Narrative Silence
2. Numerical Anomaly
3. Cross-Chapter Contradiction
4. Institutional Violation
5. Causal Reversal
6. Source Transmission Distortion

The best lead topics usually combine at least two anomaly types.
